Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026. This policy applies to Cutout for Android, package dev.kigya.cutout. Contact the Cutout developer at kirylboriczewski@gmail.com.

This update describes the migrated advertising and consent flow in Cutout 1.1.7. Older installed releases may continue to use the provider selection and consent behavior included in their own binaries until they are updated; changing this page does not change an installed app.

Your images stay on your device

ISNet background removal and manual mask editing run locally. Cutout does not upload your images, image contents or clipboard contents for processing. Selecting a file grants access to that item. Copying places a result in the Android clipboard; saving writes it to your chosen destination or gallery. Other applications you paste or share to have their own privacy policies.

Local storage and permissions

Cutout stores working images, results, language, appearance and optional Bubble preferences in app storage. Temporary files are managed locally; saved gallery files remain until you delete them. App storage is excluded from Android backup. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local app data but does not delete exported gallery files. Bubble uses optional display-over-other-apps access and a foreground service. Notifications and manufacturer background settings help it remain available. Cutout does not use an accessibility service or request broad photo-library read access.

Diagnostics and configuration

Cutout uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config. The Usage analytics and Error reports switches are independent and can be changed in Settings. Analytics data is sent only when Usage analytics is enabled and applicable Google UMP Analytics-storage consent permits collection. Crashlytics follows its separate switch. Google UMP consent mode for advertising and Analytics was enabled for this app's Firebase/AdMob configuration on 3 November 2026. UMP controls applicable advertising-purpose signals; the app's Analytics switch remains an additional requirement. App-defined diagnostics exclude images, clipboard content, paths, filenames, support messages, email addresses and purchase tokens. Remote Config continues to retrieve app configuration independently of these telemetry controls. Data already received is governed by Google's policies and configured retention.

Advertising

Free Cutout may display banner and interstitial ads inside its main application. The Bubble and its overlay panel contain no ads. Google AdMob is the sole mediation platform. Depending on the server-side mediation group and provider/account eligibility, AdMob may serve Google's demand, Unity Ads through in-app bidding, or Yandex Ads through its official AdMob custom-event waterfall. The app does not choose the ad network through Remote Config. AdMob and participating providers may infer an approximate region from network information such as an IP address. Cutout does not request device-location permission and does not use a client IP-lookup service, SIM, language or time zone to choose a network.

Google UMP updates advertising privacy information on each cold start while the startup screen is visible and displays a form only when UMP says one is required. The app waits while a form is open. If the first online update or form load fails, startup continues without ad requests and a late response will not show a form over the main app. Settings offers UMP privacy options only when required. Cutout has no separate ad-consent dialog or manual personalized-ad toggle. Pro suppresses advertising.

Cutout's Android manifest includes com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID for advertising SDK use and does not request precise or approximate device-location permission. Ad SDK requests may transmit network information, including IP address, advertising/device identifiers and ad interaction or diagnostic data to Google AdMob and whichever mediated network receives a request. Cutout does not send images, clipboard contents, paths, filenames, support text or email addresses to advertising services.

At this policy's effective date, Cutout deliberately passes Unity's privacy.consent=false as a conservative fallback, so Unity mediated requests are contextual-only. UMP's granular opt-in mapping has not been verified; this policy does not say that UMP currently enables personalized Unity demand. Yandex's AdMob adapter reads applicable standard TCF/Additional Consent signals. See the provider policies for details: Google, Unity and Yandex.

When Usage analytics is enabled and applicable UMP Analytics consent permits it, Firebase Analytics automatically sends one ad_impression event for each actual impression. The app does not add a duplicate manual impression event. Separate diagnostics may describe an opportunity, request, load, error or close. AdMob's configured Yandex custom-event eCPM is a waterfall estimate, not a live auction price or confirmed payout; unknown revenue is not reported as zero. Unity earnings from in-app bidding through Google mediation are paid by Google under the publisher's account, not by Unity. See Unity's bidding and payout guide.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Cutout does not receive your payment-card details. The app uses purchase evidence to grant or restore Pro and maintains a bounded local access cache. This version does not use a subscription-verification server. Google Play controls billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds. See subscription terms.

Support

When you submit the feedback form, its message, reply email if supplied, rating value, random request ID, app/version/platform and support tier are sent via Google Apps Script to kirylboriczewski@gmail.com. Images are not attached automatically. Pro support receives priority and a reply within 24 business hours. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address the request and associated obligations; request deletion through the same contact. Duplicate request IDs are retained for up to six hours and a hash of an optional reply email for up to ten minutes for rate limits; a daily quota stores a date and count. Apps Script execution-log retention is governed by Google’s service settings. These technical records help prevent abuse.

Your choices and deletion

You can change the independent Usage analytics and Error reports switches, respond to UMP privacy forms when shown, reopen UMP privacy options in Settings when required, disable Bubble, revoke permissions, manage subscriptions in Google Play, and delete local app data. Contact support for questions or deletion requests concerning correspondence and developer-controlled data. Provider-held data and rights may depend on your region and the provider’s process.

Audience and third parties

Cutout is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If a child has submitted personal information, contact support. Google, Unity and Yandex may process network, device and advertising information under their respective privacy policies and safeguards. Pixora has a separate pre-existing Google Analytics export to Unity through BigQuery; Cutout has no additional Unity Analytics link configured. These exports are separate from the ad mediation adapters.
