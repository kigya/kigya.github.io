Cutout · Privacy · Subscriptions

Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026. This policy applies to Cutout for Android, package dev.kigya.cutout. Contact the Cutout developer at kirylboriczewski@gmail.com.

Your images stay on your device

ISNet background removal and manual mask editing run locally. Cutout does not upload your images, image contents or clipboard contents for processing. Selecting a file grants access to that item. Copying places a result in the Android clipboard; saving writes it to your chosen destination or gallery. Other applications you paste or share to have their own privacy policies.

Local storage and permissions

Cutout stores working images, results, language, appearance and optional Bubble preferences in app storage. Temporary files are managed locally; saved gallery files remain until you delete them. App storage is excluded from Android backup. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local app data but does not delete exported gallery files. Bubble uses optional display-over-other-apps access and a foreground service. Notifications and manufacturer background settings help it remain available. Cutout does not use an accessibility service or request broad photo-library read access.

Diagnostics and configuration

Cutout uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config. Usage analytics and crash reporting are enabled by default with an in-app notice; each can be disabled independently in Settings. Diagnostic data may include technical usage events, app and device information, diagnostic identifiers, and crash stack traces. App-defined telemetry excludes images, clipboard content, paths, filenames, support messages, email addresses and purchase tokens. Remote Config fetches app settings independently of the analytics and crash switches. Disabling collection stops future optional reporting; data already received is governed by Google’s policies and configured retention.

Advertising

Free Cutout may display banners and interstitial ads inside its main application. The Bubble and its overlay panel contain no ads. Unity Ads through Unity LevelPlay is the enabled advertising provider; the integration includes the LevelPlay Ad Quality service. Advertising can process advertising/device identifiers, technical app and device data, ad interactions, fraud-prevention data and impression revenue information according to provider policies. Cutout asks for a separate advertising privacy choice before requesting ads. Settings lets you change that choice. Declining personalization permits only the provider’s supported restricted advertising behavior; it is not a claim that all identifiers disappear. Analytics/crash choices remain independent. Pro suppresses ads. Test inventory and production inventory are distinguished in technical diagnostics.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Cutout does not receive your payment-card details. The app uses purchase evidence to grant or restore Pro and maintains a bounded local access cache. This version does not use a subscription-verification server. Google Play controls billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds. See subscription terms.

Support

When you submit the feedback form, its message, reply email if supplied, app/version/platform and support tier are sent via Google Apps Script to kirylboriczewski@gmail.com. Images are not attached automatically. Pro support receives priority and a reply within 24 business hours. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address the request and associated obligations; request deletion through the same contact. Technical rate limits and bounded duplicate protection help prevent abuse.

Your choices and deletion

You can disable diagnostics, change advertising privacy choices, disable Bubble, revoke permissions, manage subscriptions in Google Play, and delete local app data. Contact support for questions or deletion requests concerning correspondence and developer-controlled data. Provider-held data and rights may depend on your region and the provider’s process.

Audience and third parties

Cutout is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If a child has submitted personal information, contact support. Google and Unity may process data internationally under their respective safeguards and policies.
