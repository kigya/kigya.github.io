Cutout · Privacy · Subscriptions

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026. This policy applies to Cutout for Android, package dev.kigya.cutout. Contact the Cutout developer at kirylboriczewski@gmail.com.

This update describes new Cutout builds using AppLaunchKit 0.3. Older installed builds may retain their previous advertising providers and consent behavior until updated. This page does not alter an installed app. Google Firebase, Google Play Billing and review features remain separate from advertising mediation.

Your images stay on your device

ISNet background removal and manual mask editing run locally. Cutout does not upload your images, image contents or clipboard contents for processing. Selecting a file grants access to that item. Copying places a result in the Android clipboard; saving writes it to your chosen destination or gallery. Other applications you paste or share to have their own privacy policies.

Local storage and permissions

Cutout stores working images, results, language, appearance and optional Bubble preferences in app storage. Temporary files are managed locally; saved gallery files remain until you delete them. App storage is excluded from Android backup. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local app data but does not delete exported gallery files. Bubble uses optional display-over-other-apps access and a foreground service. Notifications and manufacturer background settings help it remain available. Cutout does not use an accessibility service or request broad photo-library read access.

Diagnostics and configuration

Cutout uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config. Usage analytics and Error reports are independent switches in Settings. Analytics follows its saved switch and Crashlytics follows its separate switch; neither changes advertising consent. App-defined telemetry excludes images, clipboard content, paths, filenames, support messages, email addresses and purchase tokens. Remote Config retrieves app configuration independently of these controls. Data already received follows Google’s policies and the configured retention.

Advertising

Free Cutout may display ads inside its main application; the Bubble and its overlay panel contain no ads. Cutout uses Unity LevelPlay to mediate banner and interstitial ads from Unity Ads and Yandex Ads. The new integration does not include Google AdMob or Google Mobile Ads. Providers participate only where their accounts and applications are approved. Network selection and audience restrictions are managed in the providers’ consoles, rather than through a client-side country lookup. The app does not request device-location permission or use SIM, language or time zone to select an ad network.

Cutout presents its own advertising privacy dialog over the startup screen before onboarding or the main app. You can allow personalized ads, allow basic ads, or decline advertising. Personalized ads permit the disclosed advertising data use for personalization; basic ads permit device access and processing needed for basic ad delivery, fraud prevention and measurement without personalized targeting. An initial decline prevents advertising SDK initialization and ad requests. Withdrawing an earlier permission stops new requests and discards prepared ads; privacy changes are also sent to SDKs already initialized in the current process. An already open ad can finish normally. These choices do not limit the app’s image-processing tools. Advertising privacy in Settings lets you reopen the dialog and change or withdraw your choice. Previously saved UMP or older manual consent is not treated as consent to the new disclosure.

Cutout’s manifest includes com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID for advertising SDK use and excludes precise and approximate device-location permissions. Eligible ad requests may send IP/network information, the Android advertising ID when available, device information and ad interaction or diagnostic data to LevelPlay and the participating ad providers. Contextual or basic ads are not necessarily identifier-free. Cutout does not send images, clipboard contents, paths, filenames, descriptions, support text or email addresses to advertising services. See the providers’ policies: Unity and Yandex.

Before advertising initialization and requests, the app passes the current advertising choice to the LevelPlay and Yandex privacy APIs. It does not create an IAB TCF consent string or infer consent from an ad-loading switch. A changed choice invalidates prepared ads; an already displayed ad retains its owner until it closes. Google UMP and InMobi CMP are not included in the new consent flow.

When Usage analytics is enabled, the app sends one Firebase ad_impression event from a LevelPlay impression-level revenue callback. It does not send an additional automatic Google Mobile Ads copy. The event may include the network, format, unit, known value and currency. Separate opportunity, request, load, error and close diagnostics are not additional impressions or revenue. An unknown amount stays unknown; known zero and estimated amounts remain distinguishable. SDK-reported values are not a guarantee of settled earnings or payment; actual credits depend on the relevant network’s reporting and agreement.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Cutout does not receive your payment-card details. The app uses purchase evidence to grant or restore Pro and maintains a bounded local access cache. This version does not use a subscription-verification server. Google Play controls billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds. See subscription terms.

Support

When you submit the feedback form, its message, reply email if supplied, rating value, random request ID, app/version/platform and support tier are sent via Google Apps Script to kirylboriczewski@gmail.com. Images are not attached automatically. Pro support receives priority and a reply within 24 business hours. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address the request and associated obligations; request deletion through the same contact. Duplicate request IDs are retained for up to six hours and a hash of an optional reply email for up to ten minutes for rate limits; a daily quota stores a date and count. Apps Script execution-log retention is governed by Google’s service settings. These technical records help prevent abuse.

Your choices and deletion

You can change Usage analytics and Error reports independently, reopen Advertising privacy in Settings to change or withdraw the ad choice, disable Bubble, revoke app permissions, manage subscriptions in Google Play, and delete app data or uninstall the app. Removing consent prevents new ad requests; provider data already received is subject to that provider’s retention and rights process.

Audience and third parties

Cutout is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If a child has submitted personal information, contact support. Google, Unity and Yandex may process network, device and advertising information under their respective privacy policies and safeguards. Pixora has a separate pre-existing Google Analytics export to Unity through BigQuery; Cutout has no additional Unity Analytics link configured. These exports are separate from the ad mediation adapters.
