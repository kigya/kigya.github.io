Subscription Terms

Cutout’s image processing, export, Bubble and manual mask editing remain available free of charge. Cutout Pro removes in-app ads and provides priority customer support with a reply within 24 business hours. It does not promise 24/7 support.

Pro is available as an automatically renewing monthly or yearly Google Play subscription without a free trial. Base US prices are US$2.49 per month and US$14.99 per year. Actual local prices, taxes and the full billing period are shown by Google Play before purchase; the Play purchase screen is authoritative. The annual plan is charged for the full year.

Payment is charged to your Google Play account. Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel through Google Play. Cancellation stops future renewal; access normally remains for the paid period, subject to Play’s status and policies. Uninstalling Cutout does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Restore purchases from Cutout using the Google account that owns the purchase. Pending or declined payments do not grant access. Offline access may require periodic reconfirmation. Complimentary access codes are distinct from purchases and do not create a renewable subscription.

Google Play handles billing and refund requests according to its applicable policies. Cutout does not collect card details. Contact kirylboriczewski@gmail.com for support. Effective 31 October 2026.
