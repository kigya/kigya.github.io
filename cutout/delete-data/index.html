Cutout data deletion requests

Cutout does not create user accounts or upload images for background removal. This page explains how to request deletion of identifiable support correspondence and what you can remove on your own device. This request channel covers support correspondence. Records held by Google Play, Firebase, or Unity may require a separate provider request.

Request deletion of support correspondence

Email kirylboriczewski@gmail.com with “Cutout data deletion request” in the subject. If you supplied a reply email in your support message, write from that address. You may include the support request ID or an approximate date to help locate it. Please provide only the minimum information needed to find the message. Do not send photos, device identifiers, advertising IDs, passwords, or payment details.

The developer can search for and delete identifiable Cutout support messages from the active support mailbox. If a message cannot be matched to the information provided, it may not be possible to identify it. Support messages remain in that mailbox until deleted. Requests are handled under applicable privacy law.

Data covered and retention

  • Support message and form details: the message text, optional reply email, app version, platform, support tier and rating are sent to the Cutout support mailbox only after you tap Send. The developer can delete an identifiable message from the active mailbox. No images are attached automatically. A request ID is used for bounded duplicate protection and is not included in the support email body.
  • Temporary abuse-prevention records: the endpoint keeps a duplicate-request ID for up to 6 hours and a one-way email hash for up to 10 minutes. A daily date and message count are retained for quota control. These records are not a support-message archive. Apps Script execution-log retention has not been independently established.
  • Crash diagnostics: Google says Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. This statement applies to Crashlytics data only; it is not a deletion period for all Firebase data.
  • Other Firebase and Unity data: their retention and ability to identify a record from an email address depend on the provider and data involved. Cutout does not receive a provider-wide deletion result through this email request.
  • Google Play billing: purchase and subscription records are managed by Google Play and are not erased by uninstalling Cutout or emailing the developer. Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play.

Remove data stored on your device

Use Android Settings to clear Cutout’s app storage or uninstall the app to remove its local working files and preferences. Images you saved or exported to Photos, Gallery, or another folder are separate files; delete those yourself in the app that stores them. Android manages clipboard contents. Clearing app storage or uninstalling Cutout does not remove exported files or Google Play billing records.

Provider requests and choices

You can turn off Analytics and Crashlytics independently in Cutout Settings and change the advertising privacy choice there. These controls affect future optional collection; they do not by themselves erase data already received by a provider.

  • Firebase privacy and security information. Google’s Firebase Installation ID documentation describes deletion of data associated with an installation ID. It also explains that deleting an installation ID does not delete Google Analytics data. Cutout does not expose an installation-ID or Analytics deletion control.
  • Unity Ads and app-user privacy policy. Where Unity’s per-app privacy disclosure offers controls, use them to review the data and request deletion of historical data. You can also contact Unity’s privacy team at DPO@unity3d.com. Unity may need information or verification under its policy; this Cutout email address cannot locate Unity records by email.
  • Google Play subscription management. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
