Pixora Pro subscription terms

Last updated: 29 October 2026. These terms describe Android Pixora Pro.

What Pro includes

Pro removes advertising, gives access to experimental offline Recreate, and provides VIP Customer Support through the existing support form. Recreate needs a compatible device, storage space and a separately downloaded model of approximately 1.88 GB. Local processing speed and results depend on your device and input. No support response time is guaranteed.

Complimentary passes

Pixora can also grant Pro through a valid signed complimentary pass. This is free access and does not create a Google Play purchase or automatically renewing subscription. Passes can be reused on compatible Pixora installations, are not tied to a device or region, and do not expire. Removing a pass does not cancel a paid subscription, and cancelling or losing a subscription does not remove a separate valid pass. Manage subscription is available for an actual Google Play subscription. Access through either source provides the same Pro features.

Content limits and reporting

Recreate blocks descriptions matching a local prohibited-term policy and provides an in-app action to report generated results. The text policy does not classify images or guarantee detection of all unsafe content. Do not use Recreate to generate prohibited content.

Billing and renewal

Paid Pro access is offered through an automatically renewing Google Play subscription, monthly or yearly. Initial US reference prices are $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year. The app and Google Play purchase screen show the actual local price, currency, billing period, taxes and applicable terms. The yearly plan shows its monthly equivalent and the full annual payment. Any savings percentage compares the annual price with twelve monthly payments, using current Google Play prices in the same currency and rounding to the nearest whole percent. The yearly price is charged as one full annual payment. There is no free trial or introductory discount in these plans.

Google Play charges the payment method associated with your Google account. Renewal continues until you cancel through Google Play. Cancelling renewal does not by itself end access for the remaining paid period. Google Play manages refunds and subscription changes under its policies.

Manage, restore or cancel

Use Manage subscription in Pixora Settings or Google Play subscriptions. Restore purchases queries the active purchases for your Play account. Pending purchases do not grant Pro until confirmed.

Offline access and retained files

A recent Google Play subscription confirmation can remain available for up to 24 hours offline. Network failures do not extend that period. Pixora has no subscription-verification backend and does not claim an independently verified exact expiry or refund status. A new check is required after the cached period. A separate valid complimentary pass continues to grant Pro offline. Access loss prevents new Recreate runs and downloads but does not delete existing results or models, or interrupt a generation already started. You can delete the model separately in Settings.

Support and privacy

Contact support at kirylboriczewski@gmail.com or through Pixora Settings. VIP labels are client-supplied and are not protected server verification of payment. Sending a message requires your explicit Send action. See the privacy policy for Billing, advertising, telemetry and support data handling.
